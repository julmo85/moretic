Digital accessibility

Is a website difficult to navigate, a document hard to read or a setting getting in the way? MoreTIC helps identify the obstacles and find practical improvements that fit the situation.

Readability, keyboard navigation, information structure or assistive settings: the review focuses on a specific real-life use. Any agreed changes are then checked in that same use.

Accessibility project in Fribourg

MoreTIC is also developing the Architectural Autonomy Without Borders project, a practical field guide designed to document real-world accessibility in Fribourg more clearly.

The idea is to bring existing information together, identify what is missing and prepare pilot guides covering routes, accessible toilets, disabled parking, cultural venues and practical barriers.

Français English Deutsch

An independent, citizen-led and evolving project, seeking collaboration with disabled people and others directly concerned, associations, institutions and public authorities. This initiative is separate from MoreTIC’s commercial services.
